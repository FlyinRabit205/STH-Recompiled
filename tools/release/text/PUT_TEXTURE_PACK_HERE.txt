Optional HD textures: drop the "Shadow the Hedgehog HD" texture pack zip (shadow_the_hedgehog_hd.zip,
the Dolphin pack) into this folder, or its extracted folder.

The next start converts it once (about a minute) into textures\replacement\pack_import.pak.
After that the zip can be deleted. To remove the HD textures, delete pack_import.pak and pack_import.txt
in textures\replacement.

The texture pack is made by its own author and is not part of this port. Get it from where its author shares it.
